# Supervisor Quick Guide

## 🎯 Your Role
- **Review** students' weekly reports within 24 hours.
- **Provide feedback** on their progress, challenges, and plans.

---

## 💬 How to Provide Feedback
#### **0. Prerequisites**
- Ensure you have a **GitHub account** and are logged in.

#### **1. Open the Email from GitHub**
- Locate the **GitHub notification email** in your inbox.
- Click the **link to the repository** mentioned in the email.


#### **2. Navigate to the Weekly Report**
- In the repository, find the **`week-XX.md`** file for the current week (e.g., `week-01.md`).
- Click on the file to open it in the GitHub interface.

#### **3. Edit the File**
- Click the **pencil (✏️) icon** in the top-right corner of the file to open the GitHub editor.

#### **4. Add Your Feedback**
- Read through the student's report.
- Scroll down to the **` 💬 Supervisor Feedback`** section.
- Replace the placeholder text with your feedback.

#### **5. Save Your Changes**
- Click the **green "Commit changes" button**.
- Add a **commit message** (e.g., `Added feedback for week-01`).
- Keep the default option **"Commit directly to the `xxxx` branch"** selected.
- Click **Commit changes** to save your feedback.

---
That’s it! The student will receive a notification that you’ve added feedback to their report.
